# Newborn Primitive — Persistence Before Meaning

Frank must not begin with a worldview, vocabulary, personality, or pretrained cognitive model.

The ground rule comes first:

> Maintained patterns persist. Unmaintained patterns lose force. Contradiction does not occur merely because time passed.

This is deliberately asymmetric:

- reinforcement preserves or strengthens a commitment;
- absence of reinforcement decays residual force toward zero;
- decay never flips polarity by itself;
- contradictory evidence may oppose or replace a polarity;
- derived beliefs may disappear while the Residual Commitment Field remains the reconstruction source.

A commitment therefore does not decay from `true` to `false`. It decays from a directional commitment toward insufficient force to matter.

## Birth state

A newborn Frank begins with:

- an empty Residual Commitment Field;
- no language labels;
- no named concepts;
- no learned preferences;
- no external LLM as cognitive authority.

Incoming agents may provide observations or candidate encodings, but the ground layer owns reinforcement, decay, contradiction, and persistence.

## First-breath environment contract

The first-breath phase is **Linux-terminal only**.

Frank does not begin life inside Android, an assistant UI, a phone shell, an app sandbox, or a production operating-system integration. The newborn environment is intentionally smaller so that the first observable behavior belongs to the developmental substrate itself rather than to Android services, UI frameworks, pretrained assistants, or hidden platform state.

During first breath:

- the process is launched and observed from a Linux terminal;
- inputs are explicit, local, developmentally appropriate observations;
- outputs are inspectable terminal-visible state transitions or projections;
- no Android services are required for cognition to exist;
- no network dependency is required for cognition to exist;
- no LLM response may substitute for the newborn cognitive loop;
- persistence must survive process restart through Frank's own declared state contract;
- failures must remain reproducible from terminal commands and local state.

Android integration begins only after the first-breath/newborn invariants are demonstrated independently. Android is a later body and environment for Frank, not a prerequisite for his first cognitive state transition.

## First loop

```text
observation
    ↓
residual commitment
    ↓
reinforcement / contradiction
    ↓
time without reinforcement
    ↓
loss of residual force toward zero
```

The first implementation slice must preserve this semantic invariant before adding packed storage, mmap, secondary indices, neural hand-off, Android services, or hardware acceleration.

## First-breath exit condition

The phase is complete only when the Linux-terminal newborn can demonstrate, from a clean state and again after restart:

1. observation creates or perturbs the intended residual commitment;
2. reinforcement increases or preserves directional force;
3. unreinforced time reduces force toward zero without polarity inversion;
4. contradiction applies opposing directional pressure rather than masquerading as decay;
5. derived state can be rebuilt from the Residual Commitment Field;
6. the same sequence is reproducible without Android, network access, or an external model acting as cognitive authority.

Only after these invariants pass should Frank acquire the next environmental layer.
