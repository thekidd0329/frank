# Newborn Primitive — Persistence Before Meaning

Frank must not begin with a worldview, vocabulary, personality, or pretrained cognitive model.

The ground rule comes first:

> Maintained patterns persist. Unmaintained patterns lose force. Contradiction does not occur merely because time passed.

This is deliberately asymmetric:

- reinforcement preserves or strengthens a commitment;
- absence of reinforcement decays residual force toward zero;
- decay never flips polarity by itself;
- contradictory evidence may oppose or replace a polarity;
- derived beliefs may disappear while the Residual Commitment Field remains the reconstruction source.

A commitment therefore does not decay from `true` to `false`. It decays from a directional commitment toward insufficient force to matter.

## Birth state

A newborn Frank begins with:

- an empty Residual Commitment Field;
- no language labels;
- no named concepts;
- no learned preferences;
- no external LLM as cognitive authority.

Incoming agents may provide observations or candidate encodings, but the ground layer owns reinforcement, decay, contradiction, and persistence.

## First loop

```text
observation
    ↓
residual commitment
    ↓
reinforcement / contradiction
    ↓
time without reinforcement
    ↓
loss of residual force toward zero
```

The first Rust slice should preserve this semantic invariant before adding packed storage, mmap, secondary indices, or neural hand-off.
